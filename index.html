About me

I'm Jeanne, an electrical and electronics engineering student based in Nairobi. This site tracks what I actually picked up during my embedded systems training — the parts that stuck, the bugs that ate my evenings, and the boards that eventually cooperated.

It's built the way I wish I'd documented it the first time: one continuous log instead of scattered notes, written after the fact but covering everything from the electronics fundamentals to the networked builds near the end. CAPSTONE · URBAN FLOOD MONITORING

FloodSentinel

An early-warning system for urban flooding. An ESP32 reads water levels off HC-SR04 ultrasonic sensors placed at flood-prone points, and when levels cross a threshold, a SIM800L GSM module fires off alerts — no WiFi or campus network required, just a SIM card and signal.
